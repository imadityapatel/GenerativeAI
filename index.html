Applied Case Studies

Use these scenarios to practice requirements analysis, architecture, evaluation, risk assessment, and responsible deployment.

University Student Support Assistant

A university wants a grounded assistant to answer questions about admissions, courses, examination rules, and campus services.

Requirements and design considerations

  • Stakeholders: students, faculty, registrar, IT and privacy officers.
  • Use approved policies and current university documents as the knowledge base.
  • Retrieve evidence and cite the source document and effective date.
  • Escalate personal records, ambiguous policy questions, and high-impact decisions to authorized staff.

Key risks

  • Unsupported policy answers
  • Outdated documents
  • Personal data exposure
  • Peak-period availability

Case study activity

Create a RAG architecture, source freshness policy, 20-question evaluation set, and escalation matrix.

Healthcare Information Navigator

A clinic explores a tool that explains approved patient education material without replacing clinicians.

Requirements and design considerations

  • Limit the system to approved educational content and clearly defined use cases.
  • Do not present it as a diagnostic or emergency service.
  • Use conservative responses, visible limitations, and clinician escalation.
  • Protect patient data and avoid sending identifiable information to unapproved services.

Key risks

  • Medical misinformation
  • Sensitive data leakage
  • False reassurance
  • Accessibility and language quality

Case study activity

Develop a safety case, red-team scenarios, refusal/escalation criteria, and clinical review process.

Enterprise Knowledge Assistant

An organization wants employees to search internal policies, manuals, and knowledge articles.

Requirements and design considerations

  • Respect access permissions from the source systems.
  • Track document owners, versions, and retirement dates.
  • Use hybrid retrieval and reranking where justified.
  • Return citations and say when evidence is insufficient.

Key risks

  • Cross-team access leakage
  • Conflicting versions
  • Low retrieval recall
  • Sensitive internal content

Case study activity

Design permission-aware retrieval, an evaluation dashboard, and a content-governance workflow.

Marketing Content Studio

A marketing team wants draft copy, campaign variants, and localized content while maintaining brand consistency.

Requirements and design considerations

  • Define approved brand voice, claims, and prohibited content.
  • Require review for factual, regulated, or comparative claims.
  • Track source assets and usage rights.
  • Measure edit effort, factual errors, and brand adherence.

Key risks

  • Unverified claims
  • Copyright and licensing
  • Brand inconsistency
  • Hidden demographic bias

Case study activity

Create a prompt specification, human review checklist, and campaign quality rubric.

Customer Service Agent

A company wants an assistant to answer questions and use narrowly scoped tools to check order status or initiate low-risk workflows.

Requirements and design considerations

  • Separate answer-only requests from actions that modify records.
  • Use strict tool schemas, identity checks, and least privilege.
  • Require confirmation for cancellations, refunds, or irreversible actions.
  • Maintain trace logs and safe fallback paths.

Key risks

  • Prompt injection
  • Unauthorized actions
  • Incorrect tool arguments
  • Poor escalation

Case study activity

Draw the agent state machine, tool permission table, and human approval gates.

Multimodal Learning Companion

An educational service wants to explain diagrams, summarize notes, and create accessible study materials from approved resources.

Requirements and design considerations

  • Support text and image inputs with clear source attribution.
  • Check generated summaries against original material.
  • Provide accessible alternatives and avoid fabricated citations.
  • Keep learner data private and make educator review possible.

Key risks

  • Incorrect visual interpretation
  • Accessibility gaps
  • Copyright concerns
  • Learner privacy

Case study activity

Prepare a multimodal pipeline, accessibility rubric, and grounded-output evaluation set.
